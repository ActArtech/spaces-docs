# Follow along — owner week loop

Use this with the live product: https://spaces.keyteller.com/studio/

Demo logins live in [USER-GUIDE.md](./USER-GUIDE.md). Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) after a deploy.

## The loop (one sitting)

| # | Job | Where | Done when |
|---|-----|-------|-----------|
| 1 | See what needs me | **Home** action list | You can open the top item |
| 2 | Win or open work | **Pipeline** then **Projects** | Job Order `YYMM-NNNN` exists |
| 3 | Staff the job | Project **People** tab | Lead + roster rows show |
| 4 | Assign delivery | **Work** | Task has Lead (first) + Support |
| 5 | Talk on the job | Project **Activity** | Note saved; `@` only for real records |
| 6 | Invoice / bill | Project **Money** or **Finance** | Sales Invoice or Purchase Invoice |
| 7 | Collect / pay | **Payments** | Payment Entry allocated to the invoice |
| 8 | Check load | **Capacity** | Job scale set; Add/Adjust persists |
| 9 | Evidence papers | Project **Docs** | Checklist ticks + optional URLs |
| 10 | Stuck or wrong? | [Report an issue](https://github.com/ActArtech/spaces-docs/issues/new/choose) | Component + situation filled |

## Words that matter

| Say | Means |
|-----|-------|
| Job | Live delivery project |
| Job Order | Human number on the job |
| People | Who is on this job |
| Work | Tasks |
| Money | Invoices and bills for the job |
| Payments | Cash in and out |
| Docs | Evidence checklist (not the ledger) |

Full glossary: [PLATFORM-GLOSSARY-AND-FUNCTIONS.md](../product/PLATFORM-GLOSSARY-AND-FUNCTIONS.md) or the [PDF](../exports/Spaces-Platform-Glossary.pdf).

## What is not daily product UX

**Desk** (`/desk`) is infrastructure and rare setup (print-format design, fiscal year, freeze/merge, salary structure). Do not train the week loop through Desk.
